# Coffee Empire — prompts de génération du comptoir

Étape 02 : demande utilisateur « un comptoir face au joueur », pour une maquette statique. Génération via l’outil imagegen intégré, pas via CLI/API. Les images de référence guident le style ; elles ne sont pas des cibles de retouche.

Les prompts ci-dessous sont ceux réellement envoyés. Les exigences restent séparées : décor sans tasse centrale, puis sprite sur fond transparent ; l’interface est construite en HTML/CSS.

## Décor — fond opaque

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original background layer for a 2D coffee clicker/tycoon browser game, Coffee Empire. Input images: image 1 approved game logo (style and warm coffee palette only, do not redraw or include it); images 2-4 style references only, not edit targets. Create a wide 16:9 landscape, front-on eye-level view of a small cozy artisan coffee shop counter, facing the player. Carefully drawn premium retro pixel art with a consistent visibly stepped pixel grid and restrained dithering, in the same visual family as the logo and references, not smooth vector art. Warm wooden service counter stretches horizontally across the full foreground. Its countertop is a shallow horizontal plane at 70%-78% of image height, its paneled front fills the bottom 22%. IMPORTANT keep a broad uncluttered empty countertop landing area at the exact center for a separate oversized cup sprite to be placed later; absolutely no cup, mug, saucer, steam or coffee bean object in the central 40% of the picture. Back wall warm cream plaster, simple wooden shelves with a few coffee jars; espresso machine to one side on a rear counter; soft pendant lamps; small window giving a hint of muted cool dusk for contrast. Balanced modest artisan café, not luxurious or busy. Keep central back wall low-detail and unobstructed to make an overlay cup readable. Color palette espresso #2D211C, coffee #684834, caramel #A8754F, latte #C9A77D, cream #E8D9C5, amber #D2A14A, very restrained muted sage. Warm amber lighting and deep brown shadows, hand-crafted texture; no neon. Fill the entire rectangular canvas; no outer frame. No people, no UI, no text, no letters, no numbers, no menu signage, no logos, no watermark. Not isometric, not top-down, not photorealistic, not a 3D render. This is only the environment layer, not a completed game screenshot.

## Tasse — fond transparent

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: standalone transparent sprite, the main coffee cup for Coffee Empire, a 2D retro coffee clicker game. Input images: image 1 approved Coffee Empire logo, use its CUP as the exact visual design anchor, not its typography or crown; images 2-4 style references only. Create one large cream/caramel ceramic cup filled with dark espresso, standing on a matching shallow saucer, seen nearly front-on at counter eye level with only a small glimpse of the oval top. A dark espresso coffee-bean emblem on the front, handle to the right, warm amber highlights, espresso-brown outline and shadows, restrained pixel dithering. Keep the design consistent with the approved logo cup. Three modest wisps of warm steam, NO crown, no star or jewel. Crisp deliberate retro pixel art, visibly stepped edges, coherent pixel size, not smooth vector, not photorealistic, not 3D. Centered isolated cup and saucer, tight balanced framing with about 8% empty transparent margin, strong silhouette. Genuine transparent background everywhere outside sprite, preserve transparent alpha around steam and handle hole. No rectangular background, no checkerboard baked into artwork, no surrounding scene, no lettering or wordmark, no loose coffee beans, no leaves, no decorative sparkles, no watermark, no extra items. This is a reusable foreground object separate from a coffee-shop counter background.
